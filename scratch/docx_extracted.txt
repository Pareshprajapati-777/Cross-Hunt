1. Project Scope

This document separates the Indian marine-tourism market into two products: (A) PRIVATE CHARTER, where a customer hires an entire boat/yacht/vessel, and (B) PUBLIC / SHARED TOUR, where customers purchase individual seats or cabins. Large ocean-going cruise ships are generally sold per passenger or via charter enquiry; they should not be represented as ordinary hourly boat rentals.

3. Route Map for the Project

Goa: Panaji / Mandovi River → sunset, dinner, sightseeing, Aguada/dolphin products; private yacht itineraries are operator-dependent.

Mumbai: Mumbai cruise terminal → Mumbai round trips; Mumbai → Goa; Mumbai → Kochi; Mumbai/Goa → Agatti/Lakshadweep on scheduled Cordelia sailings.

Kochi: Marine Drive → Kochi harbour; public lunch/sunset/dinner cruises; whole-vessel private charter up to 150 guests; scheduled cruise sectors to Kavaratti/Lakshadweep.

Andaman: Port Blair → Havelock/Swaraj Dweep; Havelock → Neil/Shaheed Dweep; private charter routes to Ross Island, Corbyn's Cove, Elephant Beach, Turtle Beach and longer island routes.

Chennai: yacht/boat charter market exists, but public prices and capacities are less consistently published online than Goa, Kochi and Andaman. Treat unverified listings as QUOTE_ON_REQUEST.

4. Pricing & Booking Logic

PRIVATE: display the total boat price first. Example: ₹30,000 / 2.5 hours for a private Andaman charter, not ₹2,500 per person.

PUBLIC: display per-person or per-cabin pricing. Example: Cordelia is a cruise booking product where fare depends on sailing date and cabin.

Always store tax and permit charges separately. Andaman Yacht Rentals states 18% GST and permit charges of ₹500 per Indian and ₹2,500 per foreign guest on its listed charter package.

For live inventory, never hard-code a price permanently. Save source_price, currency, price_unit, valid_from, valid_to and last_verified_at.

For quote-based operators, show 'Price on Request' instead of inventing a rate.

5. Product UI Recommendation for Cross-Hunt

Search mode 1: PRIVATE CHARTER — choose city, date, passengers, duration and vessel size.

Search mode 2: PUBLIC TOUR — choose departure, destination, date and passengers; show available sailings.

Card badges: PRIVATE, PUBLIC, PER PERSON, WHOLE BOAT, GST EXTRA, PERMIT EXTRA, LIVE AVAILABILITY.

Filters: capacity, price, duration, departure port, destination, vessel size, AC, food, music/DJ, fishing, sightseeing and overnight.

Booking states: AVAILABLE, REQUEST_QUOTE, SOLD_OUT, WEATHER_HOLD, NOT_SCHEDULED.

6. Important Accuracy Rules

Prices and schedules are dynamic. The figures in this document are published examples captured from public sources on 1 October 2026, not guaranteed future booking quotes. For production use, Cross-Hunt should re-check the operator source before showing a final payable price.

A cruise ship, inter-island ferry and private yacht are different inventory classes. Do not merge them into one generic 'ship' category in the database.
